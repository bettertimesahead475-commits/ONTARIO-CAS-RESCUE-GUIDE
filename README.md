# CYFSA Ontario – Parent Education & Document Analyzer  
**Educational Platform (Not Legal Advice)**

## Overview
This project is a multi-section educational web platform for Ontario parents involved with the Child, Youth and Family Services Act (CYFSA) and related Family Court processes.  
It provides structured education, procedure walkthroughs, document explainers, and a document analyzer.  
This platform does not provide legal advice and relies only on verified primary Ontario/Canada sources.

## Core Sections (Separate Page Identities)

### 1. CYFSA Guide (Ontario Only)
- Emergency removal process (first 5 days)  
- Grounds for protection under CYFSA  
- CYFSA worker authority limits  
- Parent rights and child rights  
- Evidence vs hearsay vs opinion  
- Procedural timelines  
- Fact vs fiction  
- Watch-for checklist: assumptions, contradictions, missing evidence, unverified claims  
- Hyperlinked primary sources per section  

### 2. Family Court Process (Ontario)
- Types of family court proceedings  
- Temporary vs final orders  
- Case conferences, motions, status hearings  
- Affidavits  
- 5-day rule  
- Evidence handling  
- Court document lifecycle  
- Hyperlinks to official Ontario court forms  

### 3. Child Development Impact (Evidence-Based)
- Short-term and long-term impacts of removal  
- Attachment disruption  
- Trauma research  
- Reunification outcomes  
- Peer-reviewed research summaries only  

### 4. Document Analyzer (Educational)
The analyzer flags:
- Missing legal thresholds  
- Unsupported claims  
- Hearsay  
- Opinion vs fact  
- Procedural violations  
- Timeline inconsistencies  
- Charter and human rights references  
- CYFSA authority limits  

Supported file types:
- PDF  
- DOCX  
- TXT  
- Images (OCR)

Analyzer output:
- Red flags  
- Source-linked explanations  
- What to verify  
- What to ask a lawyer  
- What is missing  

### 5. Auto-Fill Document Templates (Educational Drafts Only)
- Draft affidavit builder (non-court)  
- Case timeline builder  
- Evidence log  
- Issue summary sheet  
- Parent preparation worksheet  
- Export to PDF  

These are educational drafts and not court-ready forms.

### 6. Voice Assistant (Accessibility)
- Text-to-speech  
- Speech-to-text  
- Voice commands  
- Human-sounding narration  
- Read-aloud mode  
- Section summaries
